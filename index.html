// common questions

Building your first app should feel clear.

Can I build an app without coding knowledge?

Yes. You bring the business knowledge and customer insight; we guide the product strategy, app design, development and launch in plain language.

How do I turn my business idea into an app?

We begin with the problem, audience and smallest useful solution. Then we shape a roadmap and prototype before writing production code.

What is the difference between an MVP and a full app?

An MVP contains the smallest useful feature set needed to test the idea with real users. A full app usually adds more workflows, integrations and scale after the core idea has been validated.
