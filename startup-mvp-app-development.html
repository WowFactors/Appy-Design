MVP questions

What is the difference between an MVP and a full app?

An MVP is a useful first version built to validate the core problem and solution. A full app typically adds secondary workflows, advanced integrations and scale after evidence shows what users need.

Can I build an app without coding knowledge?

Yes. We explain decisions in plain language and use visual prototypes so you can confidently lead the business side while our team handles design and development.

Can every MVP launch in six weeks?

No. A focused product may fit that timeline, but complex integrations, multiple user roles or regulated workflows can require longer. We confirm scope and timing after discovery.
